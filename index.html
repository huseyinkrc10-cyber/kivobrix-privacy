This policy applies to the Android version of Kivobrix.

2. Locally stored game data

The game may save scores, records, level and challenge progress, in-game currency or rewards, achievements, saved sessions, and settings such as language, sound, and visual themes on your device. These data support gameplay and preserve progress.

The current game does not require a Kivobrix-specific online account to play, and the developer does not operate a separate game-account server for this local progress. Device-level backup services may handle some local game data depending on your operating system settings.

3. Advertising and third-party data processing

Ad-supported versions of Kivobrix may use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK to show optional rewarded ads and ads at natural transitions. Ads require an internet connection; the core game can be played offline.

Google advertising technologies may collect or share IP addresses (which can be used to infer approximate location), advertising identifiers and other device/app identifiers, app and ad interaction data, performance information, and diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service operation. Actual handling can vary by device settings, region, SDK features, and privacy choices.

Data may be transmitted to Google systems. Learn more at Google Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and advertising choices

Where required, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) may ask you to choose your preferences for advertising and associated data processing. You may accept, decline, or manage choices where those options are offered. Where legally required, an in-app privacy-options entry allows you to review your choices later.

Declining advertising consent does not prevent you from playing the core game. However, optional reward-based features may be unavailable if an appropriate ad cannot be loaded. No ad reward is granted unless the required ad viewing is completed.

5. Support emails

If you email us, your email address and any details you voluntarily provide are used to process your question and reply. The email service provider may also process the communication to provide its service. Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to resolve the inquiry and meet applicable legal obligations.

6. Security, retention, and deletion

Local game progress is stored on your device, where Android handles device security and backup settings. Google Mobile Ads Next-Gen SDK encrypts data in transit using TLS. No electronic system can be guaranteed entirely risk-free.

Because there is no separate developer-operated online player-account database for local progress, remote deletion of such local data is not applicable. You can clear app data in Android settings or uninstall Kivobrix. Device or platform backups may be subject to separate retention rules.

7. Your privacy rights

To request access, correction, deletion, or exercise other rights available under applicable law, email kivobrix.support@gmail.com. We will evaluate requests concerning data under our control, subject to applicable law. For data independently processed by Google, Google's privacy policies and controls also apply.

8. Children's privacy

Kivobrix does not directly ask players to enter their name, postal address, or telephone number. Ad-related age targeting and child-directed protections will be configured according to the audience and the applicable Google Play requirements for the released version. Parents or guardians may contact us with concerns.

9. Policy updates

We may update this policy if app functionality, SDKs, or data handling practices change. The current text and update date will be posted on this page.
